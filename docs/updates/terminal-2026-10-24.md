# Terminal update — October 24, 2026

[Open Terminal](https://parallaxlaunch.live/terminal) · [Selected browser source](../../platform-updates/terminal-2026-10-24/) · [Repository](https://github.com/0xMichealDev/Parallax)

This records the hosted Terminal improvements through version 179, application revision `ccd95fe82ca8ec566e005c36d47ee9003f23c279`. It is a dated release record; provider data and live market counts change.

## What shipped

- A full-width desktop workspace with discovery on the left, a larger chart in the center and trading controls on the right. PARALLAX keeps its midnight-blue styling and branding.
- Compact token rows with project pictures, age, valuation, 24-hour volume and bonding progress. Selected-market information exposes liquidity and holders when the source supplies them.
- A reversible chart-focus mode and a collapsible research workspace for positions, fills, pulse and evidence.
- Expanded, paginated discovery for trending, new, pre-bonding and near-bonding markets, with deduplication by mint and pool. Discovery loads at least three pages or until 40 matching markets are found, subject to available pages and the ten-page limit.
- Editable minimum valuation and 24-hour volume filters. Defaults are $7,000 valuation and $10,000 volume; zero disables each threshold. No maximum valuation is applied by default.
- Quick-buy amount presets, exact SOL amount handling, balance-based sell percentages and an embedded Jupiter quote-review flow. Opening a form does not automatically connect a wallet; transaction execution still requires wallet approval.
- Activity windows for 5 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours and 24 hours, with source timestamps. Available project images appear in discovery and selected-token views.

## Discovery and data boundaries

Pre-bonding discovery checks both new pools and active Pump.fun candidates. The first page can inspect 40 candidates and subsequent pages up to 20 each, across at most ten pages. Candidates are checked against their exact mint and pool; unknown or completed bonding states are excluded. Near-bonding uses the verified pre-bonding feed with progress of at least 70%.

These are provider-backed candidate lists, not an exhaustive index of every Solana token or a copied competitor feed. Default filters can exclude otherwise valid markets. Pagination, provider quotas, cooldowns and source availability affect the list.

Market cap is shown when supplied; an FDV fallback is explicitly labeled. Missing metrics remain unavailable rather than being invented or treated as zero. Holder metadata may lag. Matching secondary-source data can fill missing liquidity, while a reported zero remains zero.

The browser polls while visible. This is not a streaming ingestion system. First-page provider caching is approximately 60–120 seconds; deeper discovery pages and holder metadata may be cached for 15 minutes. Timestamps and stale-data states matter when assessing freshness.

## Verification

The hosted implementation passed its Terminal UI, pre-bonding service, worker and saved-market checks, plus the build. The UI harness exercises pagination, deduplication, refresh and late-response behavior; service checks cover page bounds, provider cooldowns and strict pool matching.

The public snapshot includes nine reproducible offline checks covering exact amount conversion, malformed amount rejection, keyboard tab navigation, deep links and chart-focus restoration. Its hash verifier checks each exported file against the recorded source manifest. Run `npm run check:terminal` from the repository root. These checks do not validate live market accuracy or execute trades.

Published desktop browser checks confirmed the larger chart, populated discovery lists, project images, chart fallback, research disclosure and Jupiter quote form. At a 1363 × 936 viewport, the native chart measured approximately 559 pixels high, compared with approximately 316 before the repair. Counts varied while testing and are not coverage guarantees.

A signed transaction and real-device mobile validation were not performed for this release.
