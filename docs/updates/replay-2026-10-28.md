## Rewind and inspect a selected market

Choose a token address or the PRLX, SHEEPLE or GATTO shortcut and select a 1, 6 or 24 hour window. Replay synchronizes completed USD candle closes with the loaded swaps. Playback supports pause, rewind, scrubbing and 0.5× through 8× speeds. A candle close becomes visible after its interval has ended.

Visitors can filter buys/sells, follow a transaction sender, jump to the largest loaded buy/sell with known USD volume, and open the underlying transaction or address in Solscan. The sender table totals only the swaps loaded through the playhead. Unknown USD values display as unknown; partially known totals are marked explicitly. Net tokens means bought minus sold in those swaps, not an account balance, cost basis or profit.

The page is available from shared navigation, the homepage tool directory, Terminal, the PRLX Singularity and curated project worlds. Terminal links preserve the selected token and pool. Refreshing or changing a replay window keeps that pool; choosing a different token clears the previous pool.

## Share captured evidence

Share links preserve the captured dataset, playhead and sender/side filters. A saved snapshot retains its original seven-day lifetime; sharing it again does not restart that clock. The UI displays its actual expiry. Opening a saved link reads that capture without refreshing upstream data. An unavailable or expired snapshot stays explicit, and Retry repeats the same request. Load latest deliberately requests a new recent capture. The evidence can also be downloaded as JSON.

## What the first release covers

The ordinary indexed pool feed returns up to **300 recent swaps within the preceding 24 hours**. A busy pool can exhaust that cap well before the requested window begins. Replay shows the loaded count, first/last trade timestamps, cap truncation, missing values, selected pool, source and capture time. Chart history and trade coverage are separate, and a chart outage does not invent candles.

Swap sides are relative to the requested token's actual input/output leg. Distinct swaps in a single transaction remain distinct. Provider-flagged anomalous records are excluded. A transaction sender can be a router or shared account. Public project-wallet website associations, when available, describe the label observed at capture and do not establish current ownership or token safety.

Transfers, liquidity changes, other-pool aggregation, complete historical wallet profit/loss and longer Gold history are future scope. No wallet connection or transaction is required to inspect a replay.
