# PARALLAX site refresh — v262

The homepage brings token lookup, Project Atlas and Earthbound together. The workspace menu groups research and world destinations, and the dashboard offers direct paths to Wallet Watch, Replay and the Earthbound lobby.

Hosted version 262 uses native source commit `1cd0a4cdd4adf56a15ce0386d5fe96149befd091`.

From the homepage, visitors can look up a Solana contract, visit Atlas Station or choose an Earthbound PvP match or Free Roam. The shared tool finder opens with quick links and lets visitors browse the full directory or search by name. Research, creator, account and help pages use shorter headings and introductions.

On compact screens the Atlas station starts with its rendered preview. Visitors can choose **View in 3D** to load the model. The community and solar scenes also defer drawing until their views are active. Nicole’s video remains available on the dashboard beside its skip link.

The focused public checks cover the navigation directory, Atlas loading and visibility, community destinations and Creator Studio. The public snapshot includes the six existing Atlas models and their preview images to run those checks.
