# PARALLAX Multicam — v264

PARALLAX Multicam lets creators align two recordings by sound, cut between cameras and export a highlight on their own device.

Hosted version 264 uses native source commit `0177febbbc00fee34bf0fe8ebe7ad5dc6c783ab5`.

Open the editor from the homepage or dashboard. Try the included two-angle demo or choose two local clips. The editor supports manual sync adjustments, a cut list, landscape and portrait framing, and exports up to 30 seconds in a video format supported by your browser.
