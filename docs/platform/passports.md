# Project Passports

Collect, compare, share and verify a research snapshot.

[Live guide](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/passports) · [Guide index](README.md)

A Passport saves the evidence available for a token at a particular time, with source links, retrieval status and an export fingerprint.

## Collect and read

1. Open [Project Passports](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control) and enter the full token address.
1. Collect a report, then inspect the time, chain data, selected market pair and evidence gaps.
1. Save the report to your account. Collect a fresh report when you need current data.

Indexed prices and liquidity describe the selected pair. They are not necessarily totals across every market. Missing sources are shown as gaps.

## Compare two snapshots

Compare reports for the same token and network. A market-pair change can make price or liquidity comparisons inappropriate; unavailable or incompatible values should not become numerical changes. A difference between rolling 24-hour volume readings is not trading volume during the interval.

## Share deliberately

Use the report’s share control to create a public link. Review the report and any included summary before sharing. Anyone with the link can read the shared content. Your other reports, watchlists and private Assistant chats are not included.

Revoking the link stops future access through that link. It cannot retrieve downloaded copies or screenshots. Linked creator identities are current contextual information, separate from the historical snapshot.

## Export and verify

Download Passport JSON, then use [Verify an export](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control#verify) to check its fingerprint. A matching SHA-256 fingerprint confirms that the checked content matches the recorded export format. It does not certify the token, prove the source data true, or create an on-chain audit.
