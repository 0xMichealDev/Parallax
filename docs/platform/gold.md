# Gold membership

Eligibility, monitoring, and your personal research assistant.

[Live guide](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/gold) · [Guide index](README.md)

Gold is the holder-access tier of Parallax Pro. Verify a wallet holding at least **100,000 PRLX** to unlock the current Gold tools.

## Activate your membership

1. Open [Parallax Pro](https://parallaxlaunch.live/pro) and sign in.
1. Connect the wallet you want to verify and review the ownership-signature request.
1. Recheck holdings. Eligible members see Gold styling and an active membership badge.

Only eligible tokens held directly in the verified wallet count. Tokens held in locks or custody do not count toward the threshold. Access is rechecked and can stop if the balance or wallet proof no longer qualifies. No staking or token transfer is required.

## What Gold includes

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Gold Assistant | Explain a saved Passport, compare two snapshots of the same token, ask follow-up questions and revisit saved conversations. |
| Pro monitoring workspace | Monitor up to five wallets and five project tokens per scan, review in-page change notices, and export the latest monitored snapshot. |
| Membership identity | Gold site accents, active Pro badge and shareable membership-card presentation. |

## Ask better research questions

Start with “What evidence is missing?”, “What changed between these snapshots?” or “Explain this liquidity figure.” The assistant uses the reports you select. Its answers are explanations of historical evidence, not a live market feed.

AI explanations include source references, but a reference does not guarantee every interpretation is correct. Read the underlying source and collection time. The assistant does not execute trades or control your wallet.

## Current limits

Gold has a shared allowance of 20 AI generation attempts per account per UTC day across Assistant, Passport summaries and comparison explanations. Attempts can count even when a request fails. Shared service capacity also applies; the interface explains when a limit is reached. Saved conversations remain separate from public Passport shares.

Pro change notices compare successful refreshes in the current page session. Optional automatic refresh runs while the page is visible. Background Telegram alerts are configured separately in their supported tools; opening Pro does not enable them.

## Separate paid features

Premium Worlds and planet growth purchases are separate from Gold membership. Holding PRLX does not represent equity, a guaranteed return or a claim on project revenue.
