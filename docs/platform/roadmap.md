# What’s live. What’s next.

A status-led roadmap with clear boundaries.

[Live guide](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/roadmap) · [Guide index](README.md)

This is a snapshot of delivery status as of October 20, 2026. Planned items are directions, not release-date commitments.

## Live

Available Project Passports with sharing and export checks; Wallet Watch and supported Telegram rules; Gold holder access, Pro monitoring and Gold Assistant; Creator Studio and Atlas; SolHandle identity lookup and display.

Individual features still depend on source availability, account eligibility and the limits shown in the interface. Deployed does not mean independently audited.

## Validation in progress

Needs live checks Joint SolHandle SDK review, real ownership-transfer testing and iPhone/Phantom wallet signing. Real-device Telegram delivery and Gold model-response walkthroughs remain important end-to-end checks alongside automated tests.

## Planned

Future scope Partner Mint inside PARALLAX, expanded Gold research tools, and further AI Mission Control workflows. These are not currently available merely because they appear here.

## Follow changes

Read the [release notes](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/releases) for documented product changes. The broader [Flight Plan](https://parallaxlaunch.live/roadmap) covers the project’s creative and exploration direction.
