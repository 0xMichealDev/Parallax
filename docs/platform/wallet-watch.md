# Wallet Watch & alerts

Follow an address and understand its activity.

[Live guide](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/wallet-watch) · [Guide index](README.md)

Inspect a Solana address or resolve an @handle, then keep the wallet you reviewed in your watchlist.

## Inspect and save a wallet

1. Open [Wallet Watch](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control#wallet) and enter a complete wallet address or SolHandle.
1. Review the full resolved address, SOL balance, transaction references and data availability.
1. Save the wallet with a label you recognize. Your personal label is separate from its verified current SolHandle.

When saving an @handle, ownership is checked again against the address you reviewed. If it changed, inspect the new result before saving. An existing watch follows its recorded address after a handle transfer.

## Read activity carefully

A transaction reference does not by itself identify a buy, sale, profit or wallet owner. A token transfer is not automatically a sale. Wallet snapshots can be partial when a provider is unavailable; those gaps remain visible.

## Enable Telegram separately

Saving a wallet does not switch on alerts. Connect the supported Telegram destination, check the connection status, and explicitly enable the relevant rule. Review whether the destination is private or public before enabling delivery. Public community rules require explicit consent.

Delivery depends on monitoring coverage, provider callbacks, scheduled recovery and Telegram availability. Use the tool’s test and health controls to confirm your setup; a saved rule alone is not proof of delivery. Limits and available destinations are shown in the interface.

## Market alerts and Pro notices

The Observatory’s supported price/liquidity alert pilot is configured independently. Pro monitoring notices compare visible-page refreshes and are not background Telegram notifications. Choose the workflow for the delivery you actually need.
