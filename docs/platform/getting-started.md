# Start with one question

Find a project, collect evidence, and return with context.

[Live guide](https://parallaxlaunch.live/docs/getting-started) · [Guide index](README.md)

PARALLAX is a Solana research and creator workspace. Use it to inspect projects, follow wallet activity, save research and publish a home for your project.

## Your first research session

1. Copy the token’s full Solana contract address from a source you trust. A ticker alone can refer to multiple tokens.
1. Open [Project Passports](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control), sign in when prompted, and collect a report for that address.
1. Read the collection time, selected market pair, sources and missing-data notices before drawing a conclusion.
1. Save the Passport. Collect another later to compare what changed.

## Choose the right tool

| Your question | Open |
| --- | --- |
| What does the available token data show? | [Project Passports](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control) |
| What activity references this wallet? | [Wallet Watch](https://parallaxlaunch.live/control#wallet) |
| What changed across my saved research? | [Gold Assistant](https://parallaxlaunch.live/assistant) |
| Where can I explore community projects? | [Project Atlas](https://parallaxlaunch.live/planets) |
| How do I publish my project’s world? | [Creator Studio](https://parallaxlaunch.live/studio) |

## Accounts and wallet access

Public pages can be explored without Gold. Saving private research and using account features may require sign-in. Wallet verification uses a signed message to prove control. Gold eligibility is checked from the verified wallet’s eligible PRLX balance.

Gold verification does not require staking, sending tokens or approving a token transfer. Premium Worlds purchases and trading have separate transaction flows.

## When something is unavailable

A missing value means the source could not provide verified data for that request; it does not mean zero. Retry the relevant tool and keep the collection time in view. On iPhone, if Safari cannot connect Phantom, open the site inside Phantom’s browser.
