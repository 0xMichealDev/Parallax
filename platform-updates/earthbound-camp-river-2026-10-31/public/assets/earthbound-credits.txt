PARALLAX Earthbound — visual sources

Celestial panorama: ESO/S. Brunier, "The Milky Way panorama", eso0932a.
https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Adaptations: JPEG compression, spherical reprojection, artistic orientation,
exposure adjustment, atmospheric blending. This is not a current star chart.

Ground diffuse and OpenGL normal texture: Gravelly Sand, Dario Barresi / Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/gravelly_sand
CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
Adaptations: JPEG compression, tiling and shader color/lighting adjustments.

Moon: NASA/JPL/USGS, Galileo, PIA00405.
https://science.nasa.gov/resource/earths-moon/
Jupiter: enhanced image by Kevin M. Gill (CC BY), based on images courtesy of
NASA/JPL-Caltech/SwRI/MSSS, PIA22946.
https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA22946
Saturn: NASA/JPL-Caltech/SSI, Cassini, PIA17172.
https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA17172
Other telescope observations are credited individually in the eyepiece.

Terrain, lighting, campsite, satellites, meteors and audio are simulations.
Twilight horizon, creosote and canvas textures are generated art.
Credits identify source authors and do not imply endorsement of PARALLAX.

Launch campus: a fictional fan-built environment, with artist-designed black
livery and stylized rocket dimensions. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX.
Vehicle silhouette references: https://www.spacex.com/vehicles/falcon-9/
and https://new.spacex.com/vehicles/starship
Bark diffuse texture: Bark Brown 02, Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven (CC0).
https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02
Adaptations: atlas packing, UV mapping, color and lighting adjustments.

SpaceX logo by SpaceX, via Wikimedia Commons (PD-textlogo):
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SpaceX_logo_black.svg
Vector source mirror: https://github.com/twyleg/constructions/blob/d25b7431593143fe59d7ca9420ce2152a04647f1/starship/resources/SpaceX_logo_black.svg
Letter paths restored to their original horizontal positions, view box cropped.
The logo is a trademark of SpaceX. Display color is inverted on the sign.

Expedition tent, camp chairs/cooking table and footbridge: generated with Meshy.
Embedded PBR maps resized for browser delivery; bridge and tent fitted to the scene.
Task and packaging provenance: /assets/scenery/{tent,camp,bridge}/provenance.json.
