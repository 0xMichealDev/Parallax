Evidence, privacy & your allowance ＋

Your evidence

Selected Passport data, your question, your saved research focus, and the last six completed exchanges are sent to OpenAI to generate an answer. Source links open the provider’s current page; snapshot timestamps describe the evidence used for the answer.

This assistant does not fetch live news, track developer wallets, execute trades, or monitor projects. Missing information remains unknown.

Your conversations

Conversations are saved privately in your Parallax account until you delete them. Export or delete your history even if your Gold membership expires.

Deleting a Passport does not delete existing conversations; it prevents new answers that require that report.

Your allowance

20 requests per account per UTC day, shared with AI Passport summaries and change explanations, subject to shared capacity. Failed generation attempts may count. Each conversation supports 30 requests.

No automatic AI requests or background charges.
