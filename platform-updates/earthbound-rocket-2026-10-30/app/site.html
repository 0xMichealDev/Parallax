      Hypothetical scenarios — SOL units, no live market data
      How the simulation works

      We model a hypothetical constant-product pool, x × y = k. Token reserve x is total supply × pool allocation. SOL reserve y is the SOL deposited. Net buy b = gross buy × (1 − fee / 100). Tokens received = x × b / (y + b). Ending spot = (y + b) / (x − tokens received). Starting implied fully diluted valuation = starting spot × total supply.

      The model removes fees before the swap. It is not a Pump.fun bonding-curve simulation or a quote from a live exchange. It excludes gas, transfer taxes, priority fees, MEV, routing, other trades, and liquidity changes. SOL is used as a unit; no dollar price is assumed. It does not predict demand, profitability, or launch success.
