Membership questions Eligibility, verification & holdings
How do I activate Pro?

Sign in, connect Phantom, and sign a wallet ownership message. Hold at least 100,000 of the official PRLX token directly in that verified wallet. Once the balance check succeeds, your Pro badge and gold outline activate. Your tokens stay in your wallet; no staking, transfer, or spending approval is required.

Will the requirement change if the price rises?

The current requirement is 100,000 PRLX. It does not automatically adjust with price. We may review the threshold as Parallax grows, but no adjustment is scheduled. Any future change will be announced ahead of time.

When are my holdings checked?

Holdings are checked when you open Pro, recheck holdings, or refresh the monitor. While this page is visible, active memberships are also rechecked about every 60 seconds. Ownership proofs expire after one hour, when you will need to verify again. The last successful check appears in your membership panel.

What if I sell, transfer tokens, or switch wallets?

If your verified balance falls below 100,000 PRLX, Pro access and Gold styling turn off at the next check. Switching or disconnecting your wallet clears the active membership display. Verify your selected wallet and recheck holdings to restore access if it qualifies.

Which tokens count toward my balance?

Only the official PRLX held directly in your verified wallet counts. Token accounts belonging to that wallet are combined. Tokens in other wallets, lock contracts, or custodial services do not count.

Why does my membership say it is not verified?

Sign in and verify your wallet, then select Recheck holdings. If a balance check fails, access stays unverified until a successful check; an unavailable balance is not treated as zero. On mobile, use Open in Phantom mobile to connect and sign.
