Sources & coverage+

Discovery uses indexed pool snapshots. It can lag new launches and does not include every Solana token.

MCAP is reported market capitalization. When it is unavailable, a separately labeled FDV can be shown. Select “Market cap only” in a column’s filters to require a reported market cap.

Pool age is the creation time of that trading pool. “Completed” is the provider’s curve-completion time when supplied; it is not an exact migration timestamp. Holder counts and reported project links can update less often than market data.

Each column keeps its own filters on this device. Filters and sorting apply to loaded pools. The first page refreshes while this page is visible; loaded older pages may be less recent. Hovering over or focusing a list holds its updates in place.
