NEW
Now · Terminal beta

Discover. Inspect. Trade.

A market workspace with context.

See the milestone +

PARALLAX Terminal combines CoinGecko trending and new-pool discovery, source-linked token research, 15-minute pool candles, largest-account concentration checks, and Jupiter’s embedded wallet-approved swap interface. Shared data caching, provider cooldowns and visible retrieval times help keep discovery and charts reliable. Older snapshots remain clearly labeled when a provider is unavailable. Save and follow a token directly in Terminal, reopen your private Saved projects list, and compare saved evidence through Your Briefing. Wallet Watch and wallet replay accept SolHandle @handles, check current ownership on Solana, and display the resolved wallet address. Saved watches stay attached to that address if a handle changes owners. Wallet replay shows supported, provider-decoded finalized swaps for a selected token, with execution ratios, timestamps, separate network fees and transaction links.

Beta coverage: replay samples up to 100 decoded swaps and supports simple SOL, USDC and USDT pairs. It does not calculate complete wallet profit or simulate a strategy. Swap routes depend on Jupiter; a new pool is not a verified graduation. Real-device wallet execution still needs validation. Practice competitions, complete-history replay, strategy testing and paid Terminal plans remain future work.

Now · Opt-in beta

Wallet Watch delivery.

Connect the wallets you choose.

See the milestone +

Enable a saved wallet rule to register and verify its monitoring subscription. Native SOL transfer matches can reach your linked private Telegram or an owner-managed community channel. Delivery history, test receipts, pause controls and queue recovery are available; recent matches also appear in Your Briefing.

Rollout checks: verify a real transfer reaches the intended destination and complete real-device checks. Delivery runs on incoming callbacks, explicit recovery, and an authenticated scheduled queue check approximately every five minutes. Definite Telegram cooldowns are retried within the attempt limit; unconfirmed sends are not automatically resent. Transfers are not classified as buys or sells.

NEW
Now · Opt-in pilot

Price & liquidity alerts.

Follow changes while you are away.

See the milestone +

My Observatory supports one price rule and one liquidity rule per pilot account, with separate token addresses, directions, thresholds and pause controls. Scheduled checks run approximately every five minutes. Matches save before-and-after evidence and can reach your explicitly linked private Telegram, with source pool links and retrieval times.

The pilot remains capped at 20 accounts. Each rule has a one-hour notification cooldown. Comparisons use the same indexed pool; a changed pool pauses the rule and long data gaps reset its reference. Missing prices are not treated as zero. Delivery and provider delays can occur. No new alerts are enabled by this update.
