How these numbers work

Visits are browser sessions separated by 30 minutes without a recorded page visit or website click. Returning visits come from a browser seen previously on that project. Browser counts are estimates, not verified people. Signed-in owner visits and recognized bots are excluded; some automated traffic may still appear. Duplicate events are filtered and unusually high activity is capped.

Only public world and mission pages are measured. First-party browser identifiers are hashed separately for each project. No IP addresses, wallet addresses, or browsing histories are stored in these analytics tables. Browser storage resets, blocked tracking, and privacy choices affect counts. Dates use UTC; the dashboard covers the latest 90 days.

Atlas Most Explored counts at most one visit per browser per UTC day, so its totals differ from these session counts. Manage your listing to opt in to public discovery counts. Only visits after enrollment qualify.

Attribution and measurement

Campaign tags and broad referrer categories describe the first recorded source in a project visit. Full referrer URLs are never stored. Old visits show “Not recorded”; private browsers and apps can hide referrals. These are recorded sessions, not verified people or purchases. Repeated clicks never increase the click rate above 100%. Campaign names are public URL labels: do not include names, email addresses or wallet details.

Weekly summaries are available here on demand. No messages are sent automatically. Historical traffic is not backfilled.
