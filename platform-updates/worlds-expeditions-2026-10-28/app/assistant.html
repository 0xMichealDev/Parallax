How your assistant uses evidence and memory

Selected Passport data, your question, your saved research focus, and the last six completed exchanges are sent to OpenAI to generate an answer. Conversations are saved privately in your Parallax account until you delete them. Export or delete your history even if your Gold membership expires. Deleting a Passport does not delete existing conversations; it prevents new answers that require that report.

AI can make mistakes. Source links open the provider’s current page; the timestamps and saved Passport describe the evidence used for the answer. This release does not fetch live news, track developer wallets, verify holder concentration, execute trades, or monitor projects for you. Missing information remains unknown.

Beta allowance: 20 requests per account per UTC day shared with AI Passport summaries and change explanations, subject to shared capacity. Failed generation attempts may count toward usage. Each conversation supports 30 requests. No automatic AI requests or background charges.
