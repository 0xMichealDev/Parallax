FOUR HOOVES. A WHOLE MOON.

Make yourself
at home.

Walk, jump, baa
Click the world, then use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Space jumps. B baas. On a phone, hold the direction pad and use the Jump and Baa buttons. Your first Baa enables sound. The sound button beside Baa and Jump lets you mute or enable it.
Look around
Drag the world to turn the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Your sheep moves in the direction you are looking.
Three little discoveries
Baa beside the flock. Jump through three glowing rings, then press E at the signal station. Jump beneath the golden bell beyond the arch. Choose a discovery to mark the way.
Meet your hosts
Walk up to the information board near the landing spot and press E, or choose Read the field guide, to learn about Sheeple and find the community.
Bring home a memory
Finish the discoveries and collect your stamp. Photo mode lets you frame and save a picture of your sheep. Return to landing brings you safely back without losing discoveries.
