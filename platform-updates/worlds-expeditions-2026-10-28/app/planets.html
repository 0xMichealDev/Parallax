How discovery works

Most Explored: listed projects whose creators opted in, ordered by counted visits during the trailing seven days. At most one visit per browser, per project, per UTC day counts, beginning when the creator enables public counts. The window uses the time shown below the results; Refresh starts a new window. Equal totals are ordered by project name, then project ID.

Only visits to public project world and mission pages count. Signed-in owner visits, recognized bots, duplicate sessions and browser privacy opt-outs are excluded. No IP addresses or wallet addresses are added to the activity records. Public results show aggregate counts, never browser identifiers or private campaign data.

These are limited browser visit counts, not verified people, token performance or safety scores. Storage resets, multiple browsers and unrecognized automation can affect them. There are no rewards tied to rankings. Curated showcase planets are not measured by this system.

New Discoveries: community listings ordered by their original project publication date. Reclaiming a spot does not change that date.
