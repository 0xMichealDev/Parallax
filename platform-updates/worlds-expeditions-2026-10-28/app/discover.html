BUILT FOR CURIOSITY

A record of what you’ve explored.

Your profile is private. Achievements recognize use of the research tools; they do not rate a project or certify investment expertise.

What do XP and badges unlock?

They record completion of these introductory missions. Finish all three to earn the Pathfinder badge and 100 XP. XP is non-transferable, has no monetary value, and cannot be redeemed for PRLX, premium access, or an airdrop.

Will my earlier work count?

Existing saved Passports and followed projects are recognized when you open your profile. Recorded reviews from Saved projects also count. If an earlier comparison was not recorded, open it again to earn the achievement. Recognition dates show when achievements were saved, not when older research was first created.

Do I need to pay or connect a wallet?

No. Sign in with ChatGPT to save progress. Research uses the existing free allowances; these missions never buy tokens, request a wallet signature, or run background scans.

What happens after I finish?

Keep using Saved projects to revisit the evidence and read creator announcements. Earned badges stay on your profile if you later remove a follow or delete a report. Repeat actions do not add more XP.
