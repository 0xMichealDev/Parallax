PILOT / BACKGROUND MONITORING

Know when price or liquidity changes.

A 20-person pilot. One price rule and one liquidity rule per account. Choose the same token or different tokens. Scheduled checks approximately every five minutes, with up to one alert per rule per hour. Delivery and provider delays can occur. No token purchase required.

Checking monitor status…

1. Connect your Telegram

2. Choose your rule

3. Review the evidence

Thresholds compare against the saved reference, which resets after a match, after 24 hours, or after a data gap over 15 minutes. Each rule compares the same indexed pool. A changed pool pauses that rule for your review. Missing data does not count as zero. Indexed prices are not guaranteed execution prices; USD liquidity changes do not establish deposits or withdrawals.

Your recent market alerts

Pilot participation and feedback are linked to your signed-in account and visible to the project owner. Leaving removes your pilot feedback and pauses your rules; delivery receipts remain in your account. An alert already sending may finish.
