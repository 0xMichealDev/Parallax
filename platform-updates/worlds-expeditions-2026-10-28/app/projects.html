Before you launch.

Do I need a token or a wallet?

A token is optional. Anyone can browse. To publish and manage a page, sign in and verify your creator wallet with a message signature. Standard publishing does not ask you to approve a payment transaction.

What does creator verification mean?

It confirms control of the signing wallet for that account. It does not establish a unique person, prove project claims, or certify a token’s safety. Your creator wallet address is public on the project page.

How do Project Passports fit in?

Token projects can add an address to their Atlas listing so visitors can open token research. Saved research snapshots remain separate from creator claims. SolHandle creator identity linking is a planned pilot, not a live feature of this release.
