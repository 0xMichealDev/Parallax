# Earthbound weapons and loadout

Adds the SPECTRE scoped precision rifle and VECTOR sidearm alongside the existing PARALLAX AR. L opens Loadout; choose AR or sniper as the primary, with the sidearm always included. 1/2 select slots, the mouse wheel or controller RB switches weapons. Existing right mouse/LT aim, left mouse/RT fire and R/controller X reload remain.

The server owns weapon selection, ammunition, fire rate, projectile damage and reload completion. Loadouts are locked after a bounty round begins; free exploration permits primary changes in protected areas. Switching cannot refill ammunition and has a draw delay. The sniper uses 5 rounds, 75 game damage and a 3.5× view; sidearm uses 12 rounds and 20 game damage. These are fictional gameplay balance values.

Meshy generated and textured both props for 60 credits total (20 geometry + 10 texture each). Blender 4.5 reduced them to 24,000 and 18,000 triangles, aligned their viewmodels and reduced PBR texture sizes. Runtime recoil, weapon drawing, reload motion, sidearm slide movement, individual muzzle anchors and distinct synthesized sound profiles are included. The scope uses a magnified world view and reticle overlay.

Validation: focused automated combat, bounty, input, sound and model-decoding tests pass. Browser visual verification was interrupted by Computer Use; the owner explicitly requested publication for their own in-game review. Visual alignment, moving-part quality and sound perception are not claimed as visually or audibly verified. Practice bounty remains free credits only, with no real PRLX deposits or payouts.
